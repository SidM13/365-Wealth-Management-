National Pension System (NPS)

A long-term, market-linked retirement savings framework

NPS is a voluntary, defined-contribution retirement scheme regulated by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA).

Contributions are invested by professional pension fund managers across asset classes during your working years. At retirement, the accumulated corpus is used partly for withdrawal and partly to purchase an annuity that provides pension income, in proportions governed by PFRDA's prevailing rules.

Rules change

Withdrawal limits, annuity requirements, age criteria, exit options and tax benefits under NPS are set by PFRDA and the Income-tax Act and are revised from time to time. Please verify current rules with official PFRDA sources or speak with us before making decisions.

Retirement Income Planning

Income from many sources, organised into buckets

Retirement income can come from an appropriate combination of assets, each chosen for its role.

  • National Pension System (NPS)
  • Mutual funds
  • Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP)
  • Debt investments
  • Government-backed retirement / savings instruments, where appropriate
  • Annuity income
  • Other suitable financial assets

The bucket approach separates money by when it will be needed. Near-term income stays stable and accessible, while longer-term money has time to seek growth. Buckets are refilled periodically as part of the review.

NPS & retirement disclosure

NPS is regulated by PFRDA; returns are market-linked and not guaranteed. Withdrawal, annuity, exit and tax provisions are subject to PFRDA regulations and the Income-tax Act as amended from time to time. Annuity income depends on the annuity product and rates prevailing at the time of purchase. Retirement-income strategies described here are educational and not personalised advice. Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully.
