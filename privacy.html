Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

365 Wealth Management ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Enquiry form: name, mobile number, email, city, age group, investment requirement and any message you provide.
  • Chat assistant: the questions you type, the assistant's replies, and, if you choose to connect with our team, your name, mobile, email, city and note.
  • Technical data: basic information such as the page from which a form is submitted. We do not use advertising trackers on this website.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or support ticket and contact you as you have consented.
  • To understand your requirements and provide information about relevant services.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

To operate this website we use trusted third-party services, which process information on our behalf:

  • Form delivery (Web3Forms): delivers enquiry and ticket details to our email inbox.
  • AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude API): processes the text of chat conversations to generate responses. Please do not share sensitive information such as PAN, Aadhaar, bank or folio details in the chat.
  • Website hosting (Netlify) and fonts/libraries (Google Fonts, cdnjs).

Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your rights
