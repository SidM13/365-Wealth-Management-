SEBI-regulated

Portfolio Management Services (PMS)

Professionally managed portfolios of securities held directly in the investor's own demat account, run by SEBI-registered portfolio managers.

PMS offers a customised or model-based portfolio managed under a defined strategy, with greater concentration and transparency at the individual-security level than a pooled fund. Services may be discretionary, non-discretionary or advisory, depending on the provider.

May suit

  • Experienced, high-net-worth investors
  • Investors comfortable with concentrated strategies
  • Long investment horizons

Consider

  • Higher minimum investment set by SEBI
  • Fee structures (fixed / performance-linked)
  • Strategy, manager track record and risk

Eligibility & risk disclosure

PMS is regulated under the SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations. Investment is subject to the minimum amount prescribed by SEBI from time to time and to the provider's eligibility norms. Portfolios are market-linked, may be concentrated and can lose value. Past performance does not indicate future returns. Read the Disclosure Document and client agreement carefully before investing.

SEBI-regulated

Alternative Investment Funds (AIF)

Privately pooled investment vehicles for sophisticated investors seeking strategies beyond traditional listed products.

AIFs are classified by SEBI into Category I (e.g. venture capital, infrastructure, SME funds), Category II (e.g. private equity, private credit, real estate funds) and Category III (e.g. long-short and other complex strategies in listed markets). Each category has its own structure, horizon and risk.

May suit

  • Sophisticated, high-net-worth investors
  • Investors seeking diversification beyond listed markets
  • Those able to commit capital for long periods

Consider

  • Lock-in periods and limited liquidity
  • Complex fee and tax structures
  • Valuation and manager-selection risk

Eligibility & risk disclosure

AIFs are regulated under the SEBI (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 2012. They are meant for eligible investors meeting SEBI's prescribed minimum investment and the fund's own criteria. AIF investments may be illiquid, carry high risk and could result in loss of capital. Read the Private Placement Memorandum carefully.

IFSCA-regulated

GIFT City Funds

Investment opportunities within India's International Financial Services Centre (IFSC) at GIFT City, Gujarat.

Funds set up in GIFT City IFSC operate under the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA). They can offer access to global and India-focused strategies, are typically denominated in foreign currency, and may be used by NRIs, foreign investors and resident Indians (within applicable remittance rules) for international diversification.

May suit

  • NRIs and globally mobile families
  • Residents seeking international diversification
  • Eligible, experienced investors

Consider

  • Currency risk and foreign-market risk
  • Remittance limits and FEMA / RBI rules
  • Tax treatment specific to IFSC structures

Eligibility & risk disclosure

GIFT City funds are subject to IFSCA regulations, applicable FEMA/RBI remittance rules and the fund's own eligibility and minimum investment requirements. Returns are exposed to market and currency fluctuations. Tax implications vary; please consult a tax adviser.

Higher risk

Unlisted Shares

Shares of companies not listed on a stock exchange, such as established private companies or businesses that may consider listing in future.

Unlisted shares can give access to companies at an earlier stage, but they carry risks that listed equities generally do not. A company may never list, timelines can change, and prices are discovered in thin, informal markets.

Key risks

  • Liquidity: you may not be able to sell when you want
  • Valuation: prices are not exchange-discovered
  • Exit: listing is never assured; lock-ins may apply after listing

Also consider

  • Limited public information and disclosures
  • Concentration in a single company
  • Specific tax treatment of unlisted equity

Risk disclosure

Unlisted shares are not traded on a recognised stock exchange and are not covered by exchange investor-protection mechanisms. There is no assurance of listing, liquidity, pre-IPO gains or any return; you may lose part or all of your investment. Suitable only for investors who understand and can bear these risks.

SEBI-regulated

InvITs & REITs

Market-based structures that give access to income-generating infrastructure and real-estate assets.

Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs) hold assets such as roads, power transmission lines and pipelines. Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) hold rent-yielding commercial properties such as office parks and malls. Units are listed on stock exchanges, and both structures are required to distribute a large share of their cash flows to unitholders, as per SEBI regulations.

Potential role

  • Diversification into real assets
  • Periodic distributions (not guaranteed)
  • Access with smaller amounts than direct property

Consider

  • Unit prices fluctuate with markets and interest rates
  • Occupancy, tariff and regulatory risks
  • Taxation varies by distribution component

Risk disclosure

InvITs and REITs are regulated by SEBI. Distributions are not guaranteed and unit prices may fall. Read the offer document and annual reports carefully before investing.

Protection

Insurance Solutions

Insurance is a foundation of financial risk management. It protects the family and the plan, rather than being a wealth-creation product.

Before investing for growth, it is prudent to protect against events that could derail financial goals: loss of income, medical emergencies and other liabilities. Adequate term life and health cover allows investments to stay invested for their intended purpose.

Protection first

  • Term life insurance for income replacement
  • Health insurance for medical costs
  • Cover reviewed as life stages change

Keep in mind

  • Insurance and investment needs are different
  • Read policy wordings, exclusions and waiting periods
  • Disclose information accurately

Disclosure

Insurance is the subject matter of solicitation. Insurance products are regulated by IRDAI and are offered only through appropriately licensed channels and partners. For details on risk factors, terms and conditions, read the sales brochure and policy document carefully before concluding a sale.
