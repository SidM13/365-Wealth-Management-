Mutual Funds

Diversified opportunities across categories and asset classes.

Mutual funds pool money from many investors into professionally managed portfolios. The right combination for you depends on your investment horizon, financial objectives and risk profile.

Fund Categories

Access a wide universe of mutual-fund categories

Each category plays a different role. Understanding that role comes before choosing a scheme.

Large Cap

Invest mainly in the largest listed companies; typically relatively less volatile within equity.

Mid Cap

Invest in mid-sized companies with growth potential and higher volatility than large caps.

Small Cap

Invest in smaller companies. Higher growth potential, higher risk; suited to long horizons.

Multi Cap

Invest across large, mid and small caps with minimum allocations to each, as per SEBI norms.

Flexi Cap

The fund manager can move freely across market capitalisations based on opportunities.

Index Funds

Passively track a market index at typically lower cost, aiming to mirror its performance.

Debt Funds

Invest in bonds and money-market instruments. Exposed to interest-rate and credit risk.

Hybrid Funds

Combine equity, debt and sometimes other assets to balance growth and stability.

Gold

Gold ETFs and fund-of-funds offer exposure to gold, often used for diversification.

Silver

Silver ETFs and fund-of-funds offer exposure to silver, a precious and industrial metal.

InvITs

Exposure to infrastructure assets such as roads and power transmission.

REITs

Exposure to rent-yielding commercial real estate through listed trusts.

Mutual Fund Portfolio Construction

Equity

Precious Metals

  • Gold
  • Silver

Fixed Income

  • Debt Funds

Relative stability and liquidity; interest-rate and credit risk apply.

Real-Asset / Yield

  • InvITs
  • REITs

Access to infrastructure and real-estate cash flows.

There is no single allocation that suits everyone

We do not prescribe one model portfolio for every investor. Allocation should reflect your circumstances, and it should be reviewed as those circumstances change.
