Our Process

A structured four-step review

Step 1 — Review

Understand

Understand existing investments and financial objectives: what you hold, why, and what each goal requires.

Step 2 — Analyse

Study

Study allocation, concentration, diversification and portfolio structure, including overlap and liquidity.

Step 3 — Allocate

Frame

Consider an appropriate asset-allocation framework according to your circumstances and risk profile.

Step 4 — Review Periodically

Track

Track whether the portfolio continues to remain aligned with your objectives as life and markets change.
