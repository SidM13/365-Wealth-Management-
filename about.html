Who We Are

A relationship-driven financial distribution and wealth-solutions practice

365 Wealth Management is a proprietorship founded and led by Pankaj Manchanda, with offices in Faridabad and Gurugram, Haryana.

We serve individuals, families, professionals, retirees and experienced investors who want more than a product recommendation. Our work begins with your goals. From there we help you understand suitable investment avenues, build diversification across asset classes and review the portfolio periodically as life and markets change.

Our philosophy is captured in four words: Plan → Invest → Grow → Secure. We do not promise returns. We aim to bring structure, transparency and discipline to how you invest.

Plan

Clarify goals, horizons, liquidity needs and risk comfort.

Invest

Choose suitable avenues and invest systematically.

Grow

Stay diversified, patient and disciplined.

Secure

Review, rebalance and protect what you've built.

Pankaj founded 365 Wealth Management to help investors make informed, structured financial decisions, with the investor's objectives always at the centre.

His approach is built on professional discipline and analytical thinking. He looks at the whole portfolio, not just the next product. He places strong emphasis on investor education: explaining how different investment categories work, what risks they carry and how they may fit together, so that investors can decide with understanding.

All services are offered within the framework permitted for an AMFI-registered mutual fund distributor. Pankaj is not a SEBI Registered Investment Adviser, and 365 Wealth Management does not provide fee-based investment advisory services.

Our Role, Clearly Defined

Transparency about what we do and what we don't

Regulation distinguishes between distribution, education and investment advice. Here is how our services fit.

We offer

Mutual Fund Distribution

As an AMFI Registered MFD (ARN 364547), we facilitate investments in mutual fund schemes and may receive commission from Asset Management Companies.

We offer

Investor Education

General educational content on investment concepts, product categories, risks and planning approaches. It is not a personal recommendation.

We offer

Portfolio Analysis

A structured review of allocation, concentration and diversification, provided incidental to our distribution services, within the permitted regulatory framework.

Not offered

Investment Advisory (RIA)

Fee-based investment advice is a separately regulated activity requiring SEBI registration as an Investment Adviser. We do not hold this registration.
