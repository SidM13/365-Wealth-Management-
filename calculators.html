About these calculators

Results are based solely on the assumptions you enter and use simplified methods: monthly compounding at one-twelfth of the annual rate for SIP/SWP/NPS, annual compounding for lump-sum and future value, and no taxes, costs or exit loads. They do not represent the performance of any scheme or product and are not investment advice. NPS annuity and withdrawal proportions are user assumptions; refer to current PFRDA regulations. Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully.
